Privacy Policy for Abyssus Drift

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Abyssus Drift (package com.tidepoollab.abyssusdrift), published by Tide Pool Lab, handles your information.

Short version

Abyssus Drift has no user accounts, no analytics and no ads, and we receive no personal data from it. The game makes no network requests of its own. Your progress never leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. Nothing the game does is sent to us, and we have no server that could receive it. The game cannot read your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files, and it does not use the Android advertising identifier.

The permissions the game holds are: VIBRATE, for short vibrations on hits and explosions, which you can turn off in the game's settings; and INTERNET, which the Unity engine declares by default. The current version of the game does not use the network.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress — the current round, your submarine's upgrades, cargo and credits — and your settings, such as language, sound and controls, to its own private storage on your device so you can return to them later. None of this leaves the device, it is not readable by other apps, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising and in-app purchases

The current version of the game shows no ads and processes no payments. Buttons for a future optional purchase may already appear in test versions; they do not charge anything.

If a future version adds ads or a purchase, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Any payment would be handled entirely by Google Play, and the game would never see your card number or billing details.

Third parties

The game sends no data to third parties. It contains no analytics SDKs, no ad SDKs and no social media integrations. Unity, the engine the game is built with, is configured not to send usage statistics.

Children

Abyssus Drift is intended for players aged 13 and over. It contains cartoon violence against vehicles and machines. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent and have a question about this game, write to us at the address below.

Data collected by Google Play

Google Play collects certain information when you download or run an app — for example install statistics and crash reports. That collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be reflected in the app's Data Safety information on Google Play.
